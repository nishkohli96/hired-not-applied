# Contact discovery system (repeatable, per company)

Fill the placeholders with the user's function, target titles and cities. Takes about 15 minutes per company.

## 1. Find the hiring manager on LinkedIn

1. Company page → **People** tab → search the function keyword (`engineering`, `product`, `security`…) → filter by location.
2. Sort the results mentally by level: the target is the person who owns the problem (1–2 levels above the role), then their direct reports.
3. Google X-ray when LinkedIn search is limited:
   - `site:linkedin.com/in "<Company>" ("Head of <Function>" OR "VP <Function>" OR "<Function> Manager") "<City>"`
   - `site:linkedin.com/in "<Company>" "<target title>"`
4. Cross-check the person is current: recent posts, "Present" on the role, company website team page, conference talk bios, press releases naming them.
5. Note one recent public, professional item per person (post, talk, article, launch) as the outreach hook.

## 2. Find the work email (verify, never guess and send)

1. **Pattern**: look for any published email at the company domain (press contact, blog author, GitHub commits, conference slides, job posts). Common patterns: `first@`, `first.last@`, `firstlast@`, `flast@`, `f.last@`.
2. **Finder tools** (free tiers are enough for a short list): Hunter.io (domain search shows the pattern), Apollo.io, RocketReach, Snov.io, Lusha, or the LinkedIn-integrated extensions of these. Use only what the tool returns as a candidate.
3. **Verify** before sending: Hunter Email Verifier, NeverBounce, ZeroBounce, or Mailmeteor's checker. Send only to "valid"; treat "accept-all/catch-all" as risky and prefer LinkedIn first.
4. If nothing verifies, use LinkedIn (connection note → DM, or InMail) instead of guessing.

## 3. Find warm paths (try before going cold)

1. LinkedIn search with **Connections: 2nd** + company filter → who you share a connection with; ask that mutual for a short intro.
2. **Shared past employers / clients**: filter the company's People by "Past company" = any of the user's past employers.
3. **Alumni**: company People tab → filter by the user's school (or the school's alumni page → "Where they work").
4. **Communities**: meetups, Slack/Discord groups, open-source projects, conferences where the company's engineers speak; engage once publicly before messaging.
5. **Investors**: VC portfolio talent pages and job boards (many funds run them) often list hiring managers or accept intros.
6. **Recruiters who place there**: agency recruiters who recently posted roles at the company.

## 4. Log and sequence

Log each contact (company, name, title, channel, hook, date) in the outreach tracker, then message in this order: warm intro → hiring manager → peer for referral → recruiter. Space follow-ups per the networking plugin's sequences.
