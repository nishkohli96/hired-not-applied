# Follow-up sequences

Day 0 = first message. Business days in the recipient's region. Voice rules: the plugin's `references/voice.md`. Every follow-up adds something new: a fresh proof point, a relevant idea, a link, an update, or a smaller ask. Never just a nudge.

## A. No reply to cold email / InMail / DM

**Day 3–4: new proof point**
> Hi {Name}, one thing I didn't mention: {P-different from first message, one line}. It's the closest thing I've done to {their must-have}. Still keen to talk about {Role} whenever you have 15 minutes.

**Day 8–10: something useful for them**
> Hi {Name}, I saw {their recent post/company news}. {One specific thought, idea or link that helps them, 1–2 lines.} If {Role} is still open, I'd welcome a short conversation. If it's filled, I'd appreciate knowing so I don't keep writing.

**Day 15–21: graceful close**
> Hi {Name}, I'll leave it here so I'm not filling your inbox. If {area} hiring opens up later, I'd be glad to be considered. Thanks for reading.

## B. Accepted connection, no reply to DM
**Day 5–7: smaller ask**
> Hi {Name}, if a call isn't practical right now, could you tell me who's leading hiring for {team}? That alone would help a lot.

## C. Applied, no response (recruiter or hiring manager)
**Day 7**
> Hi {Name}, I applied for {Role} ({Job ID}) on {date}. The short version: {2 proof points}. Is the role still active, and is there anything else you need from me?

**Day 14**
> Hi {Name}, checking on {Role}. I'm still very interested and can do a call or any assessment that helps you decide quickly.

## D. HR replied asking for details
> Thanks, {Name}. Here's what you asked for:
> - Current / last role: {title}, {Company}
> - Experience: {years} yrs ({relevant years in core skill})
> - Notice period: {immediate / X days}
> - Location: {city}; open to {remote / relocation}
> - Expected compensation: {user's stated approach, or "open to discussing based on the role's scope and band"}
> Resume attached. I'm free for a call {2–3 windows + TZ}.

## E. After a networking or informational call (same day)
> Thanks for the time today, {Name}. {Specific thing they said} was the most useful part; I'm going to {action}. {If they offered: "Here's the link to {Role} as discussed."} I'll keep you posted.

**2–4 weeks later: close the loop**
> Hi {Name}, an update: I acted on your advice about {X} and {outcome}. Thanks again.

## F. Interview thank-you (within 24 hours, one per interviewer)
**Subject:** Thanks for today, {Role}

> Hi {Name},
>
> Thanks for the conversation today. The part about {specific topic} stuck with me, and it's made me more sure this is a team I'd do good work in.
>
> {Optional: one line strengthening an answer. "On your question about {X}, I should have mentioned {proof point}; it's the closest example I have."}
>
> I'd welcome the chance to keep going with the process.
>
> Thanks,
> {First name}

## G. Post-interview silence
**5–7 business days after the stated decision date (or 7 if none given)**
> Hi {Name}, I wanted to check on timing for {Role}. I'm still keen on {team/problem}, and if there's anything else you need from me, I can turn it around quickly.

**7 more days**
> Hi {Name}, following up once more on {Role}. {Only if true: "I have another process moving with a decision due on {date}, and {Company} is my first choice."} Could you let me know where things stand?

## H. Rejection
> Thanks for letting me know, {Name}, and for the time you and the team put in. If you're able to share any feedback, I'd value it. I'd be glad to be considered for future {area} roles.

## I. Recruiter quiet after a good screen
> Hi {Name}, thanks again for the call on {date}. Is there an update on next steps for {Role}? I can make {2–3 windows + TZ} for the next round.

## J. Offer pending
> Hi {Name}, thank you for the offer. I'm going through the details and will come back to you by {date}. Could you send over {missing detail: benefits document, equity terms, start-date flexibility}?

## K. Re-engage after 2–3 months of silence (new trigger only)
Use only when something new has happened: a new role on their team, their company's news, the user's new result, or a resource they'd value.
> Hi {Name}, we last spoke about {topic} in {month}. Since then {trigger: "your team opened a {Role} role" / "I shipped {result}" / "I came across {resource} on {their challenge}"}. {One line on why it matters to them.} Would a short catch-up in the next couple of weeks be useful?

## L. Multi-channel sequence (cold contact)
Follow the "Mixing channels" table in `outreach-principles.md`. Each touch uses a different angle from the proof bank and never repeats the previous message. Example plan:
- Day 0 LinkedIn note: hook only.
- Day 2 email: hook + story-led proof + ask.
- Day 4–5 LinkedIn DM (if accepted): a different proof point + smaller ask.
- Day 8–10 value-add (sequence A, day 8–10 template) on the channel most likely to get a reply.
- Day 15–21 graceful close (sequence A) on one channel.
