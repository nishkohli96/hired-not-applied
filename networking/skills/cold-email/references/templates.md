# Cold email templates

Starting points, not scripts. Replace every `{brace}` with specifics; rewrite any line that would still make sense sent to a different company. Voice rules: the plugin's `references/voice.md`. Length 120–200 words for the engaging versions, 80–120 for the direct ones.

Proof tags like `{P-leadership}` mean "a proof-bank fact of that type, in the user's own numbers".

---

## 1. Hiring manager: the story opener (engaging)
**Subject:** {Their initiative} and the {problem} behind it

> Hi {Name},
>
> {Specific detail about their team's work, e.g. "Your team's move to server-side rendering on the listings pages"} caught my eye. We went through the same thing at {Company} last year, and the hard part wasn't {obvious part}. It was {real hard part}.
>
> I led that work. {P-leadership + P-impact as one or two plain sentences, e.g. "I ran a team of 5, rewrote the rendering layer myself, and we cut load time from 4.1s to 1.6s across 2M monthly users."}
>
> That's why the {Role} on your team stood out. {JD must-have} is the kind of problem I like owning end to end, and I've applied ({Job ID}).
>
> Would you have 15 minutes in the next week or two? I'd like to hear what the team needs most this year, and I can share what worked for us and what didn't. If someone else is running this hire, I'd be grateful for a pointer.
>
> Thanks,
> {First name}
> {Headline identity} · {LinkedIn} · {Portfolio/GitHub}

## 2. Hiring manager: direct (short)
**Subject:** {Role} · {headline proof in 4–6 words}

> Hi {Name},
>
> I've applied for the {Role} on your team ({Job ID}) and wanted you to have the short version.
>
> {Credibility line with P-leadership.} Most recently, {P-impact}. That lines up closely with {JD must-have 1} and {JD must-have 2}.
>
> Could we find 15 minutes sometime next week? Happy to work around your calendar, and if this sits with a different manager, a quick redirect would help a lot.
>
> Thanks,
> {First name}

## 3. Recruiter / HR: shortlisting
**Subject:** {Role} ({Job ID}) · {years} yrs, {core skill}

> Hi {Name},
>
> I've applied for {Role} ({Job ID}) and I think I'm a strong match, so here's what you'd want to check first:
>
> - {years} years in {core skill/domain}, {X} of them leading teams of up to {N}
> - {P-impact, one line}
> - {Must-have match, e.g. "Hands-on with Next.js, NestJS and AWS, all listed in the JD"}
>
> {Availability line, e.g. "I'm available to join immediately and open to remote or relocating to Dubai."}
>
> If it helps move things along, I can do a call any day this week. Resume attached here for convenience.
>
> Thanks,
> {Full name} · {Phone} · {LinkedIn}

(Recruiters expect the resume; attaching it here is fine. Don't attach to hiring managers or employees in the first email.)

## 4. Speculative (no open role)
**Subject:** {Area} at {Company}

> Hi {Name},
>
> {Hook about a recent move by the company, with one sentence of your own take on it.}
>
> I don't see an open {role} listing, so I'm writing to you directly. {Leader line.} {P-impact.}
>
> If you expect to grow {area} in the next few months, I'd like to be on your list. I can send a one-page note on how I'd approach {their likely challenge} so you can judge for yourself. Would that be useful?
>
> Thanks,
> {First name}

## 5. Founder / exec
**Subject:** {Specific product thing}, and a thought

> Hi {Name},
>
> I've been using {product} for {time/context}. {One honest, specific observation or idea, 1–2 sentences.}
>
> I build and lead {function}. {P-impact with business framing: revenue, cost, users, speed.} {P-leadership.}
>
> I'm looking for my next team and {Company} is high on the list. Could I take 15 minutes of your time to understand where you're headed, or is there someone on your team I should speak with instead?
>
> Thanks,
> {First name}

## 6. Proof-of-work
**Subject:** Made something for {Company}

> Hi {Name},
>
> I spent a few evenings on {a short audit / prototype / analysis} of {specific thing at their company}: {link}. The main finding: {one-line insight with a number if you have one}.
>
> {Credibility line.} I've applied for {Role}, and this felt like a better introduction than another cover letter.
>
> If any of it is useful, I'd be glad to walk you through it. Fifteen minutes would be plenty.
>
> Thanks,
> {First name}

## Closing asks that work (rotate; don't reuse the same one across a sequence)

- "Would you have 15 minutes sometime next week? I'll keep it focused."
- "Could we find a slot in the next couple of weeks? Happy to work around your calendar."
- "If a short call makes sense, tell me a time that suits and I'll make it work."
- "If you're not the right person, I'd be grateful for a pointer to whoever is."
- "Would it help if I sent a one-page summary first?"
