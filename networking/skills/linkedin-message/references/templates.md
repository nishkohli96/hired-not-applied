# LinkedIn message templates

Fill every `{brace}` with specifics. Voice rules: the plugin's `references/voice.md`. DMs 40–100 words; InMails 80–150 words.

## InMail: hiring manager, open role
**Subject:** {Role} on your team · {proof in 4–6 words}

> Hi {Name},
>
> {Specific hook on their team or work, plus one sentence of your take.}
>
> I've applied for {Role}. {Leader line or P-leadership.} {P-impact.} That maps closely to {JD must-have}.
>
> Would you have 15 minutes over the next week or two? I'd like to understand what the team needs most and share how I've handled similar problems. If someone else owns this hire, a pointer would help.
>
> Thanks,
> {First name}

## InMail: HR / recruiter
**Subject:** {Role} ({Job ID}) · {years} yrs {core skill}

> Hi {Name},
>
> I've applied for {Role} ({Job ID}). The short version of why I fit:
> - {years} yrs in {core skill}, leading teams of up to {N}
> - {P-impact}
> - {Must-have match}
>
> {Availability line.}
>
> I can do a call any day this week if that helps. My resume is on my profile, and I'm glad to send it over directly.
>
> Thanks,
> {First name}

## After connection accepted: hiring manager
> Thanks for connecting, {Name}. As mentioned, I've applied for {Role}. {One line: the proof point that matches their biggest need.} Could we set up 15 minutes when it suits you? I'd like to hear where the team is headed.

## After connection accepted: HR / recruiter
> Thanks for connecting, {Name}. I've applied for {Role} ({Job ID}): {years} yrs in {core skill}, {P-impact short}, {availability}. Is there anything else you need from me to move it forward?

## After connection accepted: internal employee (referral)
> Thanks for connecting, {Name}. I'm applying for {Role} on {team} ({link}). {P-short, ~15 words.} Would you be comfortable referring me? I've written a short summary you can paste straight into the referral form, so it takes two minutes. Completely understand if you'd rather not.

## Informational (no specific job)
> Hi {Name}, {hook}. I've spent {years} leading {area} and I'm weighing a move toward {target}. Your path from {X} to {Y} is the one I'm trying to understand. Could I ask you three questions over 15 minutes sometime?

## Re-engage old colleague
> Hi {Name}, it's been a while since {shared project}. Congrats on {their move}. I'm looking for my next {role} and {Company} is near the top of my list. Could we catch up for 15 minutes in the next couple of weeks?

## Reply to inbound recruiter: interested
> Hi {Name}, thanks for getting in touch. {Role} sounds like a good fit, particularly {detail from their message}. Quick context: {credibility line}; {availability}. I'm free {2–3 windows with time zone}. Could you share the JD and which team it sits in?

## Reply to inbound recruiter: not a fit, keep warm
> Hi {Name}, thanks for thinking of me. This one isn't the right match because {short reason}, but I'm actively looking for {target role, level, location}. If something closer comes up, I'd be glad to hear about it.

## When they say yes to a call
> Great, thank you. I'm free {window 1}, {window 2} or {window 3} ({time zone}). If none of those work, send me a time that does and I'll fit around it.
