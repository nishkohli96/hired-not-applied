# Referral templates

Voice rules: the plugin's `references/voice.md`. Make it easy to say yes: exact role, exact link, a blurb they can paste, one clear question, one graceful out.

## Cold direct ask: employee who doesn't know the user (LinkedIn DM or email)

Use when the user wants a referral from someone they've never spoken to. Common and acceptable in many markets (India and US tech especially); it works when the fit is clear and the effort for the referrer is near zero.

**Subject (email):** Referral for {Role}, {Job ID}

> Hi {Name},
>
> I'm applying for {Role} on {team} ({link}, {Job ID}) and wanted to ask you directly rather than go through the portal alone.
>
> Why I think I'm worth referring: {P-leadership}, and {P-impact}. The JD asks for {must-have 1} and {must-have 2}, both of which I've done at {scale/brand}.
>
> Would you be comfortable referring me? I've put a short summary below so it takes two minutes, and my resume is here: {link}. If you'd prefer to have a quick chat first, I'm happy to do that too. And if it's not something you can do, no hard feelings at all.
>
> Thanks,
> {First name}
>
> **Summary you can paste:** {forwardable blurb}

## Warm-up first (when the user prefers to build rapport)
> Hi {Name}, I'm looking at the {Role} on your team and your work on {project} is close to what I've been leading at {Company}. Before I apply, could I ask you two quick questions about how the team works? Ten minutes on a call or a couple of lines here would help.

Follow with the ask after the conversation:
> Thanks again for the time, {Name}. What you said about {point} made me more sure about applying. Would you be comfortable referring me for {Role} ({link})? Summary below, so it should only take a couple of minutes.

## Close friend / ex-colleague
> Hey {Name}, hope {personal/work thing they shared} is going well. I'm going for {Role} on {team} at {Company} ({link}, {Job ID}). Since we worked together on {shared project}, would you be up for referring me? Blurb and resume below so it's quick. Totally fine if it's awkward for any reason.

## Alumni / weak tie who has chatted with the user
> Hi {Name}, thanks again for the chat about {topic}; your point about {X} was useful. I've decided to apply for {Role} ({link}, {Job ID}). Would you be open to referring me as someone you've spoken with? Summary below, ready to paste.

## After they say yes
> Thank you, {Name}, I appreciate it. Here's everything in one place:
> - Role: {Role}, {Job ID}, {link}
> - Resume: {link}
> - Summary: (below)
> If the form asks for anything else, tell me and I'll send it straight over.

## Forwardable blurb (third person, ≤80 words)
> {Full name} is a {headline identity} with {years} years in {domain}. {P-leadership, third person, using their name not a pronoun}. {P-impact}. Strong fit for {Role} on {must-have 1} and {must-have 2}. {Availability line if relevant}. Resume: {link} · LinkedIn: {link}

Use the person's name instead of pronouns unless the user states theirs.

## Thank-you (after the referral goes in)
> Thanks for putting me forward, {Name}. I'll let you know how it goes.

## Later update (referrers like to hear back)
> Hi {Name}, an update on {Role}: {I have a first interview on DATE / I'm through to the final round / they went with someone else}. Thanks again for the referral. If I can ever return the favour, say the word.
