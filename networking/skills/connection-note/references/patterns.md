# Connection note patterns

Fill every `{brace}` with specifics and keep it under 200 characters. Voice rules: the plugin's `references/voice.md`. Lead with them or with a hard fact; no fluff words.

## Hiring manager: role open
> Hi {Name}, I've applied for the {Role} on your team. I {P-leadership/impact, ~10 words}. Would be good to be connected while it's in motion.

> Hi {Name}, your team's {project/launch} is close to what I led at {Company}: {result, ~8 words}. Applied for {Role} and keen to connect.

## Hiring manager: their content
> Hi {Name}, your post on {topic} matched what I saw leading {area} at {Company}, especially {specific point}. Would be good to connect.

## HR / recruiter
> Hi {Name}, I've applied for {Role} ({Job ID}). {years} yrs in {core skill}, led teams of {N}, {P-impact short}. Glad to share more whenever useful.

> Hi {Name}, {headline identity}, available {immediately/notice}. Applied for {Role} at {Company} and would appreciate being on your radar.

## Internal employee (referral path)
> Hi {Name}, I'm applying for {Role} at {Company} and your work on {team/product} is close to mine. {P-short}. Would be good to connect.

> Hi {Name}, fellow {School/ex-Company} here. Applying for {Role} on {team}; would value your view on the team. Happy to connect.

## Senior leader / founder
> Hi {Name}, {specific company milestone} is a big step. I lead {function} work, most recently {P-impact short}. Would be glad to connect.

## Met at event / community
> Hi {Name}, your point at {event} on {topic} has stayed with me. Good to stay connected.

## Career switcher
> Hi {Name}, I'm moving from {old field} into {new field}, and your path from {their background} is one I'm studying closely. Glad to connect.

## Entry level / student
> Hi {Name}, I'm a {program} student and built {project, with number}. Your work on {X} at {Company} is where I'm aiming. Would value connecting.
