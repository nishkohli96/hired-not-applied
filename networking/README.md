# networking

Get past "Apply" by reaching people directly. Every message: one hook, one ask, short, honest.

| Skill | Use it to | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| `resume-setup` | Save your resume once as `resume.md` with a proof bank; every skill reuses it | "Set up my resume" |
| `value-statement` | Find the value you keep creating across roles; get one-sentence, executive, recruiter and LinkedIn statements, evidence check and blind spots; saved to `resume.md` for all skills | "What's my value proposition?" |
| `recruiter-positioning` | Make recruiters' job easy: 50-word intro, 100-word summary, "What I do", headline, impact themes, recruiter's-eye risk check, search keywords and LinkedIn terms, call strategy, weak phrases to drop | "Prep me for a recruiter call" |
| `outreach-kit` | **Start here.** Paste resume + JD + a contact's name; get the full pack (connection note, DM/InMail, cold email, referral ask or HR fit summary, dated follow-ups) | "Here's my resume and the JD. Write outreach to Priya Sharma at Acme." |
| `coffee-chat-prep` | Full coffee-chat prep: their context and priorities, themes, goal and stance (peer/learner/collaborator/problem solver/insider), 14 tailored questions, your 30-second intro, influence signals, follow-up plan, mistakes that kill executive presence | "Prep me for a coffee chat with this person" + their profile |
| `common-ground` | Quick overlap check: shared ground rated with evidence, openers, 3 questions, credibility points | "What do I have in common with this person?" |
| `hiring-manager-brief` | Before a call or interview with a hiring manager: their pressures and priorities, hiring mindset, where you're commercially relevant, 3 questions, mistakes to avoid, tone, decision style and best positioning | "Brief me on this hiring manager" + their profile |
| `find-contacts` | Map hiring manager, recruiter, peers, alumni; search strings; email patterns to verify; a hook per person | "Who should I reach out to at Acme for this role?" |
| `connection-note` | 3 LinkedIn invite notes within the character limit | "Connection note for this recruiter" |
| `linkedin-message` | InMails, after-accept DMs, informational asks, recruiter replies | "They accepted, what do I send?" |
| `cold-email` | Work-email outreach: direct, problem-solver, speculative, founder, proof-of-work | "Cold email to the Head of Design at Acme" |
| `referral-request` | Referral ask + forwardable blurb + thank-you | "Ask Priya to refer me for this job" |
| `follow-up` | Next touch or full sequence; interview thank-yous; post-interview chasers; rejection replies | "Follow-up sequence for this email I sent Tuesday" |
| `recommendation-strategy` | Who to ask for LinkedIn recommendations or references, ranked by value; themes and talking points per person from real shared work; request message; briefing note (no ghostwriting); portfolio gaps and ideal mix | "Who should I ask for recommendations?" |
| `outreach-tracker` | Log outreach, see who's due today, weekly reply-rate summary | "Who do I follow up with today?" |

Shared references: `references/voice.md` (assertive-but-polite, human voice; banned AI phrases), `references/outreach-principles.md` (rules, channel limits, tone by domain/region, timing), `references/sender-profile.md` (proof bank of your metrics and leadership).

`scripts/voice_check.py` flags AI-sounding, weak or pushy phrasing: `python3 scripts/voice_check.py draft.txt`.

Drafts only - nothing is sent unless you explicitly ask and an email connector is set up.

To find companies worth reaching into, use `job-radar`; to decode a job and prepare the application, use `job-decode`.
