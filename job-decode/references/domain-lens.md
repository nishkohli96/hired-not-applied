# Domain, seniority and region lens

Skills in this plugin must work for any field. Detect the domain, seniority and region from the JD, then apply the matching lens.

## Domain signals and what matters most

| Domain | Hard gates to check first | What hiring managers actually weigh | Proof formats |
|---|---|---|---|
| Software / data / IT | Stack, cloud, clearance, years with core tech | Shipped systems, scale, ownership, code quality | GitHub, OSS, system design, metrics (latency, uptime, users) |
| Product / design | Domain, B2B vs B2C, tools | Outcomes, user research, decision-making | Portfolio, case studies, metric movement |
| Sales / BD / account mgmt | Territory, segment, quota history | Attainment %, deal size, cycle length | Quota numbers, logos, pipeline built |
| Marketing / growth | Channel, budget size, tools | CAC/ROAS/pipeline, experiments | Campaign results, portfolio |
| Finance / accounting | CA/CPA/CFA, standards (IFRS/GAAP), ERP | Accuracy, close speed, audit outcomes | Certifications, systems, savings |
| Healthcare / clinical | License, registration, specialty, shifts | Patient outcomes, compliance, caseload | License numbers (user provides), CME |
| Legal / compliance | Bar admission, jurisdiction, PQE | Matter types, regulators, risk outcomes | Matters handled, publications |
| Operations / supply chain | Certs (PMP, Six Sigma, CSCP), ERP | Cost, throughput, SLA, safety | Before/after metrics |
| HR / talent | SHRM/CIPD, HRIS | Hiring volume, retention, programs | Time-to-fill, engagement |
| Education / academia | Degree, teaching license, publications | Outcomes, research, grants | Publications, grants, evaluations |
| Skilled trades / engineering (non-software) | License, certifications, safety record | Project size, compliance, safety | Projects, tickets, safety stats |
| Creative / media | Portfolio, tools | Style fit, audience, deadlines | Portfolio links, reach |
| Public sector / NGO | Eligibility, clearance, KSAs | Mission fit, compliance, stakeholder work | KSA-mapped evidence |

If the field is not listed, derive the same four columns before proceeding.

## Seniority lens

| Level | JD language | What to emphasise |
|---|---|---|
| Intern / entry | "learn", "assist", "0–2 years" | Projects, coursework, internships, eagerness, potential |
| Mid | "independently", "own features" | Delivery, scope, reliability |
| Senior | "lead", "mentor", "drive", "ambiguity" | Scope, influence, judgment, measurable impact |
| Lead / manager | "manage", "hire", "roadmap", "stakeholders" | Team outcomes, hiring, strategy, cross-functional work |
| Director+ / exec | "P&L", "org", "vision", "board" | Business results, org building, strategy |

Title inflation varies by company size and region: a "Senior" at a startup may equal "Mid" at a large company. Judge by scope and responsibilities, not title.

## Region lens (resume and outreach conventions)

| Region | Resume length / format | Photo / personal details | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| US / Canada | 1 page (<10 yrs), 2 max | No photo, no DOB, no marital status | Achievement-first, ATS-friendly |
| UK / Ireland | 2 pages "CV" | No photo | Personal statement common |
| EU (DE, NL, FR…) | 1–2 pages; Europass acceptable | Photo common in DE/FR, optional elsewhere | Language levels (CEFR) |
| Gulf (UAE, KSA, Qatar…) | 2–3 pages | Photo, nationality, visa status, notice period expected | Mention availability and visa clearly |
| India | 2 pages | Photo optional; avoid DOB/marital unless asked | Notice period, current location |
| Australia / NZ | 2–3 pages | No photo | Work rights stated up front, referees section |
| Singapore / SEA | 2 pages | Photo optional | Work pass status |

Always follow the user's stated preference over these defaults.
