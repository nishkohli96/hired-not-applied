# job-decode

You have the job description. Decode it and position yourself for it. Works across domains, seniority levels and regions.

| Skill | Use it to | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| `resume-setup` | Save your resume once as `resume.md` with a proof bank; every skill reuses it | "Set up my resume" |
| `decode-jd` | Read a JD from the hiring manager's side: why the role exists, ranked priorities, business challenges, success profile, what keeps the HM up at night, weighted skills, ATS keywords, 10 likely interview questions, red flags, and how to position your resume | "Decode this JD" |
| `fit-check` | Score your match /100 with evidence, gaps, and an Apply / Network first / Skip call | "Am I a fit for this?" |
| `resume-alignment` | Check a professionally written resume against one JD without rewriting it: top 5 priorities, ranked keywords, strong / partial / missing matches, what to emphasise, supported keywords and where they fit, 5-point action plan | "Check my resume against this JD, don't rewrite it" |
| `tailor-resume` | Rewrite your real resume for one job in the right regional format | "Tailor my CV for this role, Gulf format" |
| `video-cover-letter` | 2–3 minute spoken script (280–380 words): excitement, match, relevance, one achievement, close; plus delivery coaching | "Write a video cover letter for this role" |
| `interview-answers` | JD in, Q&A sheet out: 15–18 likely questions with why they're asked, a model answer in your voice from your own resume, the follow-up to expect, and an evidence mark on every answer | "What will they ask me and what do I say?" |
| `interview-prep` | Built around one interview: predicted questions, STAR story bank, tough-question answers, questions to ask, mock interview | "Prep me for my hiring manager round" |

Shared references: `references/candidate-profile.md` (how your profile is gathered and reused), `references/domain-lens.md` (domain, seniority and region conventions), `references/output-files.md` (where drafts and briefs are saved).

To find companies worth applying to in the first place, use `job-radar`; to reach the people inside, use `networking`.

Skills read your resume from `~/.hired-not-applied/resume.md` (or `./resume.md`). Run `resume-setup` once; see the root README.

## Install

```bash
/plugin install job-decode --marketplace nishkohli96/hired-not-applied
```
